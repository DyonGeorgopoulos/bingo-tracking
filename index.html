Method. Boss and skill data comes from the Wise Old Man snapshot of the official OSRS hiscores; nearly all of them refreshed on 6–7 September 2026. Raid points weight kill counts by difficulty — Colosseum ×6, ToB Hard Mode ×4, CM ×3, ToB and Phosani ×2, CoX ×1, ToA Expert ×1.8, ToA and Nex ×0.8, Corrupted Gauntlet ×0.5. Each component is converted to a percentile across the pool, weighted, then multiplied by an activity factor: 1.00 within 2 days, 0.97 within a week, 0.88 within three weeks, 0.72 within two months, 0.50 beyond. Total XP was cross-checked against the official hiscores for the top of the board.

Captains excluded: Tuffet, Doug Lee, codeys iron, spedward, 535, J3nkos, 40k, birdman593, 1437 xo, m ass, Gay yonnis.
